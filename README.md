**Hi, I'm Ori Ben Amram**  
I'm a Computer Science and Physics student at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem, with two years of experience as a full-stack developer. I have only a few courses left, and my eyes are set on the next challenge.  

I created [RoomyGo](https://www.roomygo.com/listings), a production web and mobile platform (Next.js) for apartment and roommate matching. In short, it started with a problem I noticed among friends. I surveyed dozens of people, and the top pain point for anyone offering a room was the flood of irrelevant WhatsApp messages from applicants with no real profile. So every applicant lands on the publisher's page with a rich profile, the publisher approves only the ones they want, and the phone number is revealed only after approval.  
I'm glad it helped real people: almost 200 published apartments, and 450+ users who went through the full onboarding. The Jerusalem municipality invested funds and offered professional help to support its adoption.

Before that, I worked as a coding instructor at Coding Academy for two years, and in parallel as a developer at the MISTRBIT software house. Many of my repositories here are from that time.
